01 · Focus

Three things guide everything we make.

I.

Calm over noise

No shouting badges, no anxiety mechanics, no dark patterns. If a screen adds stress to someone's day, it does not ship.

II.

Fast and dependable

Software should answer instantly and work offline. We sweat performance and reliability the way others sweat launch-day headlines.

III.

Private by default

Your life stays yours. We design for on-device first, collect as little as possible, and never treat people as the product.

02 · The studio

Small on purpose.
Senior only.

RBH Studios is a tiny independent team. No account managers, no handoffs, no bloat. We would rather make one thing well than ten things quickly, and we plan to keep it that way as we grow.
